    How it works

    The usual scam: the creator sends SOL to fresh wallets, they buy most of the supply in the first seconds, and they sell when you arrive. Every step leaves a trace on Solana.

    1

    Investigate

    We read the token's history: the real creator, every wallet that bought in the first minute, and where each one got its SOL. Wallets funded by the creator count as the creator's network.

    2

    Sign

    The numbers are signed by the LaunchProof key in a 97-byte message that also carries a hash of all the evidence, so anyone can check it.

    3

    Check inside the purchase

    Our Solana program verifies the signature and your limits in the same transaction as the buy. If anything fails, Solana cancels the purchase.

    AI agents

    Agents connect through our MCP server. They investigate a token and buy with their own wallet, through the same guard. Even if an agent decides badly, it cannot buy a token outside its limits: the Solana program refuses the purchase.
