LaunchProof

Checks who really bought a Pump.fun token at launch, and lets you buy only if the token stays within your limits. The check runs inside the purchase transaction on Solana, so a failed check cancels the purchase.

    How it works

    1. Investigation. We read the token's history on Solana: who created it, which wallets bought in the first minute, and where each of those wallets got its SOL. Wallets funded by the creator count as the creator's network.
    2. Signed attestation. The numbers are signed by the LaunchProof key. The signed message also carries a hash of all the evidence, so anyone can check it.
    3. Check inside the purchase. The LaunchProof program on Solana verifies the signature and your limits in the same transaction as the purchase. If anything fails, Solana cancels the purchase.
